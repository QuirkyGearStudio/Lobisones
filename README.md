Quirky Gear Studio
One gear. One developer. One world full of wolves.
Quirky Gear Studio is an independent one-person game studio built on the belief that games can be funny, emotional, and beautiful all at once — regardless of the age of the person holding the screen.
I'm Sergio, a solo developer handling everything: code, art, animation, audio, and design. Not because I have to, but because I care deeply about every part of what I make. Quirky Gear is what I am today — one unconventional gear turning on its own — and it's also what I dream of becoming: a small, passionate team where every person adds something irreplaceable to the machine.
Current Project — Lobisones
Lobisones is a collection of memory and attention games set in a NaturePunk world — a colourful place where nature and steam-powered machinery coexist in unexpected and delightful ways.
The cast is a pack of wolves with wildly different personalities: energetic, shy, mischievous, wise, and everything in between. They don't just populate the game — they react, express, celebrate, and commiserate with the player. No text. No instructions. Just good, funny wolves being themselves.
Three games are in active development for iOS: Cartas, a memory match game; Pelota, a Simon Says ball-passing game; and Reunión, a sequence memory game with different scenarios.
Lobisones was designed for children and the elderly, and ended up being for everyone.
